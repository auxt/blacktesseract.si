02 · Architecture

Two layers

A sealed core that never changes. A living organism that changes every day.

The organism may rewrite almost everything about itself, except the rules it lives by. It can act only through four doors in the kernel:

submit(book)

Caps, then Monte Carlo limits scale the book before anything trades.

forge_round(…)

Every candidate is judged, and every one is written down.

deploy(name)

Only what passed the gate may go live.

retire(name)

Always allowed. Letting go is never blocked.
